## Sylva Living World, "Living Green" scene

`scene/living-green.html` is derived from `src/shaders/sylva-living-world/sources/inner-green-3d.html` in the
ThreeUI Community catalog by Meng To (https://github.com/MengTo/threeui), the free "Living Green" variant of
the Sylva Living World component (https://threeui.com/three-js/sylva-living-world/living-green). The page UI,
imagery and fonts of the source were removed so that only the procedural Three.js scene remains, the
animation loop was made to respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, and a small script was added that reports the
pointer position to the parent page.
